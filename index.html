About Lynx Combinator

Kyle Tran is the founder of SoCal's #1 youth AI bootcamp, helping students go from beginners to confident AI builders with real portfolio projects.

Most youth learn about AI in school. At Lynx Combinator, they build with it.

In 6 weeks, students go from beginner to shipping 3 real AI products: chatbots, voice agents, and customer support agents they can actually showcase.

After the program, students continue getting support from Kyle and keep lifetime access to the Lynx Combinator community, connecting them with young leaders and builders like them.

The goal is simple: help students stand out by becoming confident builders.

About Kyle

i didn't go to school for this. i taught myself — chatbots, voice agents, customer support agents, websites shipped on vercel. zapier led to chatgpt led to claude code led to realizing i could build real things that worked.

then i met a family. their kids were half my age — curious, energetic, asking questions nonstop. then something clicked.

i thought about who i was at 16. junior year. AP classes. track and field. already racing against time without knowing where i was running. nobody handed me a roadmap. i had to find that years later, alone.

those kids didn't have to wait that long.
